About Organic Farming

Organic farming focuses on producing food naturally without harmful chemicals. Farmers use compost, crop rotation, and biological pest control to maintain soil health.

This approach helps protect the environment while delivering fresh and nutritious food to consumers.

Benefits of Organic Farming

Healthy Food

Chemical-free crops that are safer and more nutritious.

Eco Friendly

Protects soil, water, and biodiversity.

Better Soil

Improves soil fertility using natural fertilizers.

Sustainable Future

Supports long-term agricultural sustainability.
